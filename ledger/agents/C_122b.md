## 3. Findings

### F1. The routing-only vs generation prefill "discrepancy" is a last-layer capture artifact, not batching, and not routing noise

Numbers (RO.json domains_summary vs GEN.json tracks.prefill.domains):
- Winner table: 18/20 domains have the same winner. Flips: linguistics (RO E9 0.009268 > E45 0.008993 >
  E233 0.008960; GEN E233 0.009138 > E9 0.009075) and philosophy (RO E72 0.009720 > E233 0.009262; GEN E233
  0.009790 > E72 0.009517). RO margins in those two domains were 0.000275 and 0.000458; GEN margins are
  0.000063 and 0.000272. These are near-ties by any reading (winner W is ~0.0090-0.0110 everywhere).
- E233 per-domain W shift GEN minus RO: mean +0.000132, range -0.000167 (statistics) to +0.000709
  (environmental_science). E233 rose in 12/20 domains.
- Overall top-12 by W: 11/12 shared (RO has E15 at #12, GEN has E20). Union of per-domain top-12 sets: RO 45
  experts, GEN 46, overlap 42. Per-domain top-12 Jaccard: 1.0 in 8 domains, 0.846 in 10, 0.714 in 2
  (comparative_religion, philosophy).
- The tell: among the 42 experts appearing in both runs' domain top-12 lists, almost every one moved DOWN
  in GEN by a nearly constant -0.00010 to -0.00018 (E45 -0.000176 over 20 domains, E215 -0.000163, E131
  -0.000165, E13 -0.000152, E9 -0.000127, E72 -0.000106, E209 -0.000107), while three moved UP: E233 +0.000132,
  E166 +0.000348 (16 domains), E21 +0.000419 (1 domain). A uniform shift of everyone plus a few risers is
  what you get when one layer's numbers are swapped out of a 48-layer average, not what random routing
  jitter looks like.
- RO.json integrity: `missing_prefill_layer_events: 60` = exactly one missing layer per cell. GEN.json:
  `missing_prefill_layer_events: 0`, `layer39_trim_events: 0`.

Mechanism (from the scripts, no tensors needed):
- The capture binary (REP/*/scripts/capture_activations.cpp lines 691-694) prefills with
  `llama_batch_get_one(tokens, n_cur)` and prefill_batch = 512, so every prompt (max ~60 tokens) is ONE batch
  in both runs. `-n` does not change prefill batching; the batch/ubatch hypothesis is ruled out by the code.
- `llama_batch_get_one` requests logits only for the last token. llama.cpp then evaluates the LAST layer only
  on output tokens, so the last MoE router (`ffn_moe_logits-47` here) sees 1 row during prefill. This is the
  known "layer 39 quirk" from the 40-layer 35B model (the 35B scripts special-case layer 39 = its last layer).
  All 122B scripts still hard-code `if layer == 39:` (analyze_domain_specialists_122b_generation.py line 174;
  analyze_hvac_6cond_l1l3.py line 161), so on 122B the quirk moved to layer 47 and nobody was checking.
- Routing-only script (analyze_domain_specialists_122b_prefill.py lines 111-113): `if arr.shape[0] < n_prompt:
  missing` -> layer 47 (1 row) is dropped in every cell -> the 60 events. RO prefill = clean mean over 47 layers.
- Generation script (lines 148-175): prefill test is `arr.shape[0] >= n_prompt`; layer 47 has n_gen+1 rows
  (1101+ rows >= 49) so it PASSES, and `prefill_logits = arr[:n_prompt]` takes 1 real prefill row plus
  n_prompt-1 GENERATION rows as "prefill". Then `arr.shape[0] == n_gen + 1` routes generation correctly via
  `arr[1:]`, but the l39 flag is only set for layer == 39, hence "0 trim events". So the GEN-run prefill is
  47 clean layers + 1 layer of mostly generation-token routing (about 1/48 = 2% contamination), and the
  generation block is fine.
- Which reading to trust: the routing-only run (E233 13/20, 7 distinct) is the cleaner prefill picture; the
  generation run's 15/20 is the contaminated one. Neither is "wrong" at the level that matters: the two
  flips are inside noise margins, the shape (one generalist wins most domains) is identical, and every
  E233 W in either run sits between 0.0089 and 0.0111.
- Evidence that would settle it without any GPU: row counts of `router/ffn_moe_logits-47.npy` in each run's
  raw cells (expected 1 in the -n 0 run and n_gen+1 in the -n 2048 run; every other layer should be n_prompt
  and n_prompt+n_gen). Those files were deleted on the box and are not on this drive. Secondary check that IS
  possible locally: nothing further; capture logs are empty.

### F4. The "trimmed" generation track is a near no-op (flag)
- The script trims at the 6-token BPE spelling of "<|im_end|>" ([27,91,316,6018,91,29]). Real spill uses the
  single special token. Result: trim_index is None for 53/60 cells; for the 7 cells where it fired, it fired
  27-53 tokens AFTER the true first special token (e.g. D03_mathematics_01 JSON 1051 vs true 998; D14_law_02
  471 vs 444) (REP/generation/raw/*/<cell>/generated_tokens.json).
- True spill (first id in {248044,248045,248046}; it is always 248044 `<|endoftext|>`, then the model keeps
  going into `<|im_start|>` loop-turns because the binary's eos_id is not 248044 for this GGUF): all 60 cells
  spill; mean generated 1868.3, mean clean-turn length 1027.4. About 45% of the "generation" block is
  post-spill text. The 18-distinct result therefore describes clean answer + spill pooled. Whether the
  dispersion survives a proper trim cannot be checked without the tensors.
- Same bug in the HVAC script (im_end_token_sequence in HVAC.json) and, by the journal's description, the
  hum/experience runs trim on decoded text instead (their trimmed lengths look right: hum 458, XP 99-2048).

## 4. Prediction scores

- P1 (35B shape; prefill concentrates on a generalist, generation disperses; 35B generalist E224 in >=70%):
  Not testable on 122B for the E224 clause. The SHAPE clause replicates on 122B: prefill 1 expert wins
  13/20 (RO) or 15/20 (GEN prefill), 6-7 distinct; generation 18 distinct, max 2 wins; Herfindahl 0.44-0.58
  vs 0.06 (RO.json, GEN.json). The 122B prefill generalist E233 is a high-S default (S 0.063, rank 1 by S),
  not a domain expert. SUPPORTED for the shape, on a different model.
- P6 (with 10+ prompts per domain, 20 distinct consolidates to 14-17; generalist wins at most 3): Not
  testable (3 prompts per domain only). Observation compatible with its spirit: 122B at n=3 already shows 18
  distinct, not 20, and the generation-side generalists E0/E11 win only 1 domain each (by S, E0 wins 3).
  MIXED-leaning-supported, weak.
- P8 (no single E114 analog in 122B; register signal carried by a small set visible mainly on the 12 softmax
  layers): First clause SUPPORTED: E114 itself falls into reflective domains (philosophy gen rank 182), and
  no single riser is reflective-specific (E40 also wins physics, E159 also wins environmental_science, E5
  rises everywhere). Second clause MIXED: the only per-expert condition effect measurable (E114 in HVAC
  L1->L3) is indeed carried mainly by softmax layers (0.68x vs 0.92x), and the experience-probe generation
  leaders split cleanly by layer type; but the strongest softmax-side candidate (E48) is a spill artifact
  (rank 1 untrimmed -> rank 9 trimmed in XP, rank 130 trimmed in HUM), and the DeltaNet side has its own
  stable generation carriers (E140, E5). "Visible mainly on softmax layers" is not established.
- P2, P3, P4, P5, P7: outside this subset (P4's "mid-pack 20-90 prefill" happens to hold for 122B E114 in
  17/20 domains, and its exception is code/law rather than reflective prompts, which is not P4's claim).

## 5. Caveats / could not verify

- No router tensors for any 122B domain run exist on this drive; all domain numbers are read from the two
  results JSONs (and were cross-checked against the .md tables, which match). The layer-47 explanation in F1
  is derived from the scripts and the binary's decode loop, not observed in tensors; the deciding file
  (`ffn_moe_logits-47.npy` row counts) is gone. The uniform -0.0001 to -0.0002 shift across experts is the
  only in-data evidence, and it fits that explanation.
- Ranks beyond the top-12 / candidate lists are unknown; "beyond lists" in F3 means > 12, not a specific rank.
- The domain generation block pools clean answers with ~45% spill text (F4). All generation-side domain
  claims are contingent on a re-analysis with proper trimming, which needs tensors we do not have.
- The experience-probe npz values are already layer-averaged; the definition "all layers pooled" is honoured
  (equal token counts per layer) but per-layer or best-layer statements cannot be made.
- Both capture logs are 0 bytes, so batch/ubatch/eos settings printed by the binary cannot be confirmed
  beyond what the run scripts and metadata JSON say.
- The baseline 150-prompt run and its 16-file Archive contribute nothing to expert specialization (no
  routing data survives); I did not re-derive its entropy/KL claims.
- HVAC and experience probe differ from the domain runs in prompt length (360-444 vs ~39 tokens); any
  prefill comparison across those runs inherits the position confound documented in
  CROSS_MODEL_POSITION_CONFOUND.md.

## 6. Summary for a lay reader

On the 122B model, when it is only reading a prompt, one expert (E233) is the top expert in most subjects
no matter the subject; when it is writing an answer, almost every subject gets its own top expert (18 different
winners across 20 subjects), and those winners mostly came from far down the reading-phase list. The two runs
that seemed to disagree about the reading phase (13 vs 15 subjects won by E233) do not really disagree: the
difference comes from the last of the 48 layers being recorded differently when the model is not asked to
write anything, a leftover from code written for the 40-layer model, and the two subjects that flipped were
ties within a hair. Two things need care before anyone quotes the 122B results: the "trimmed" numbers in
the generation run did not actually trim anything (the trimmer looked for the wrong token), so nearly half
of the "answer" text is the model babbling after it finished; and the expert E48, which the journal calls
the 122B carrier of inner-state language, is only the top expert in that babble, dropping to rank 53 (or 130
on the attention layers) inside the real answer. E114, the famous 35B expert, is a code-and-law expert in
122B and drops sharply in philosophy and psychology; no single 122B expert plays its role, though a handful
(E40, E5, E159) rise strongly into the humanities while also winning at least one science.
